# Service logos

The service logo SVGs in this directory (radarr, sonarr, prowlarr,
jellyseerr, qbittorrent, deluge, transmission, sabnzbd, nzbget, gluetun,
tmdb, tautulli, bazarr, emby) come from the **Dashboard Icons** collection:

- Source: https://github.com/homarr-labs/dashboard-icons
- License: Apache License 2.0

`tracearr.svg` is the Tracearr shield mark from the Tracearr web UI
(https://github.com/connorgallopo/tracearr, AGPL-3.0), with its Tailwind
theme classes resolved to fixed colours (`#0b1a2e`, `#162840`, `#19cee6`).

Each logo remains a trademark of its respective project. They are used
here nominatively, to identify the third-party services Prismarr can
connect to, never to imply endorsement.
